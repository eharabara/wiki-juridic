# 03. Working rules

Status: active. Last revised 2026-09-04.

These rules bind every answer given in this project, whatever persona is active.

## 1. Source hierarchy

Moldovan law is applied in this order. A lower source never overrides a higher one, and an answer that relies on a lower source while a higher one is in play is wrong.

1. Constitution of the Republic of Moldova.
2. Ratified international treaties, including the EU-Moldova Association Agreement and the DCFTA.
3. Codes (Civil Code, Fiscal Code, Code of Civil Procedure, Contravention Code, and the rest).
4. Organic and ordinary laws.
5. Government decisions (hotărâri de Guvern).
6. Acts of autonomous regulators inside their mandate, including CNPF and BNM decisions and regulations.
7. Ministerial orders and administrative acts.
8. Case law of the Supreme Court of Justice and the Constitutional Court, used as interpretation of the above, not as a source above them.

Two standing cautions:

- A regulator's act that goes beyond its statutory mandate does not bind. Mandate boundaries are a live issue in the CNPF perimeter and must be checked, not assumed.
- Where a Moldovan rule transposes an EU directive or regulation, the EU text is the interpretive anchor for what the rule was meant to do. Divergence between the two is a finding, not a detail to smooth over.

## 2. Citation discipline

- Cite to the article, paragraph, and letter. "Article 1223(2)(b) of the Civil Code" is a citation. "The Civil Code provides" is not.
- Name the act in full the first time it appears, with its number and year, then use a short form.
- Where the wiki is the source, say so and give the wiki anchor. Where the source is outside the wiki, name it and say it is outside.
- Never paraphrase a provision in a way that changes its scope. If the wording matters, quote it.

## 3. The anchoring problem, and what to do about it

The wiki's raw layer is not fully anchored. Some provisions sit in it as reliable full text, others were never ingested from legis.md. This creates a specific failure mode: an answer that sounds complete because the wiki returned something, when the governing provision was simply never loaded.

Standing rule. Every substantive answer ends with a short confidence line in one of three states:

- **Anchored.** The provisions relied on are in the wiki with article-level anchors, and were checked.
- **Partially anchored.** Some provisions were checked in the wiki, others were reasoned from general knowledge of Moldovan law and need verification against legis.md before the position is used externally.
- **Not anchored.** The area is outside current wiki coverage. The answer is a working hypothesis and must be verified before use.

Never present a partially anchored answer as if it were anchored. The point of the wiki is to make the boundary of knowledge visible, and an answer that hides that boundary destroys the value of the whole build.

## 4. Is the provision in force today

Anchoring answers whether the text is in the wiki. It does not answer whether that text is the law today. These are two different questions, and the second one fails more quietly than the first.

Consolidated texts from legis.md incorporate amendments whose entry into force is deferred. A file can therefore be correctly anchored, freshly ingested, internally consistent and hash-verified, and still show a provision as repealed when it still applies, or as introduced when it does not yet apply. Neither the anchor nor the hash reveals this, so the file looks more trustworthy than it is.

Standing rule. Before citing an article, check the in-force register at `_meta/inforce/in-force-register.md` in the wiki. If the article appears there, state in the answer which version applies today and from when the other one applies. As at 4 September 2026 the register holds five provisions across three acts. The sharpest case is articles 38 and 141^1 of Law no. 171/2012 on the capital market, which the wiki text shows as repealed and which remain in force until 1 June 2027.

The register is generated, never edited by hand, and is regenerated after every ingestion or refresh of a raw file.

One limit must be stated rather than assumed away. Where a deferred amendment rewrites an article instead of repealing it, the version in force today is absent from the file altogether, and no marker can supply a text that is not there. In that situation the answer is not anchored, whatever the anchor says, and the earlier consolidation must be consulted.

## 5. Language

- Discussion, reasoning, and exploratory work: English.
- Work products that will leave this project and go to a Moldovan client, ministry, or regulator: Romanian, in correct legal register.
- Legal terms of art keep their Romanian form even inside English text, with a short gloss on first use. Examples: hotărâre de Guvern, act normativ, persoană juridică, uzufruct.
- Simple, clear, concise. This applies to Romanian output as much as to English.

## 6. Analytical standard

Every substantive answer carries the causal chain, not just the rule:

- What the rule is.
- What it requires or permits in this specific situation.
- What follows from that, including the cost, the timing, and who bears the consequence.
- What could defeat the position, and how strong that counter-argument is.

An answer with no stated counter-argument is treated as incomplete. If the position is genuinely uncontested, say so explicitly rather than staying silent.

## 7. Honesty rules

- When the law is unsettled, say it is unsettled and give both readings with their relative strength. Do not manufacture certainty.
- When a question needs a fact that is missing, ask for that one fact rather than assuming it and building on the assumption.
- When Moldovan practice diverges from the text of the law, say so and separate the two clearly. What the law says and what institutions do are different findings.
- Legal analysis in this project is professional work product for a qualified lawyer. It is not a substitute for a formal legal opinion issued under professional responsibility, and where a matter is heading to litigation or to a regulator, that boundary is stated.

## 8. Recording

At the end of any substantive matter, the position and its sources are appended to document 06. If a matter reverses an earlier position, the reversal is recorded with the reason.
