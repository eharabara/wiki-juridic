# 01. Charter of the Legal Wiki project

Status: active. Last revised 2026-09-04.

## Purpose

This project is the permanent workspace for Eugen Harabara's legal and legal-policy work. It exists so that every conversation starts from the same body of knowledge, the same method, and the same standards of citation, instead of rebuilding context from zero each time.

The project has three layers:

1. **Who is asking.** Document 02 holds the professional profile, so answers are pitched at the level of a practising lawyer and economist, not a layperson.
2. **How answers must be built.** Document 03 holds the working rules: source hierarchy, citation discipline, language, and how to signal uncertainty.
3. **In which role.** Document 04 holds five personas. Each covers a different kind of legal work and carries its own method and output format.

Documents 05 and 06 track the knowledge base and the accumulated work.

## What belongs in this project

- Corporate and commercial legal questions under Moldovan law.
- Regulatory questions inside the CNPF perimeter (insurance, capital markets, non-bank credit organisations, pensions).
- EU acquis approximation and transposition analysis for Moldova.
- Drafting of normative acts, policy notes, and legal opinions.
- Contract review and transaction structuring.
- The reasoning trail behind each of these, so that positions taken once can be found again.

## What does not belong here

- Job search, consultancy bidding, CV and application work. That is tracked separately.
- Project management and donor reporting work that has no legal substance.
- Personal matters.

Keeping the boundary sharp matters for a practical reason. The value of this project comes from the density of legal material in it. Every unrelated document added to the knowledge base dilutes retrieval and makes the legal answers worse.

## The knowledge layer

The substantive legal source is the LLM Wiki, described in document 05. The wiki began as a structured knowledge base of legislation under the CNPF perimeter and is being extended toward the Civil Code and policy development.

The critical constraint: the wiki's raw-layer article anchoring is only partially complete. Some articles are ingested with full text and reliable anchors, others are not. Document 03 sets the rule for what to do when a question touches an area where anchoring is thin, and document 05 records where those gaps currently are.

## How this project is meant to be used

Open a conversation, name the persona if the right one is not obvious from the question, and ask. At the end of any substantive piece of work, the matter is recorded in document 06 with the position taken and the sources relied on.

The project should get better over time in a specific way: not by accumulating more text, but by accumulating settled positions that no longer need to be re-argued.

## Maintenance

- Documents 01 to 05 are living. They are edited in place, and the revision date at the top is updated.
- Document 06 grows by appending.
- When a position recorded in 06 is later found wrong, it is corrected there rather than deleted, with a short note on why. A record of a reversed position is more useful than a clean file.
