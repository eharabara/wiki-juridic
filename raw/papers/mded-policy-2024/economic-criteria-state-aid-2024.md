STATE OF PLAY REGARDING KEY INDICATORS on THE EXISTENCE OF A FUNCTIONING MARKET, Functioning of product markets , State influence on product markets Legal framework on competition and state aid In the Republic of Moldova, the Competition Law No. 183/2012 establishes the legal framework in the field of competition and regulates the protection of competition, including the prevention and counteracting of anti-competitive practices, such as: Anti-competitive agreements prohibited by Art. 5, 7, which contain the provisions of Art. 101 of the TFEU; Abuse of a dominant position prohibited by Art. 11, which contain the provisions of Art. 102 of the TFEU; Unfair competition prohibited by Art. 15-19; Carrying out mergers on the market as a tool to protect, maintain, and stimulate competition in order to promote the legitimate interests of consumers. In order to apply the Competition Law No. 183/2012, the 8 normative acts were adopted The legal framework in the field of State aid control is laid down in the Law on State Aid No. 139/2012, that reflects Articles 107 and 108 TFEU . In terms of the secondary legislation regarding State aid, 24 normative acts have been adopted, which corresponds to the vast majority of EU State aid acquis.

III. CONCLUSIONS AND FUTURE STEPS on THE EXISTENCE OF A FUNCTIONING MARKET, C onclusions There is a functional legislative framework in the field of competition and state aid. The existing laws and regulations facilitate the monitoring and control of state aid, ensuring that it does not distort competition within the market. This legislative system is essential for maintaining a level playing field for all market participants and aligns with the requirements set forth by the European Union, particularly as the country progresses in its efforts toward EU integration. Currently, there is an ongoing process to amend the secondary normative framework in the fields of competition and state aid to ensure full compliance with European Union legislation
