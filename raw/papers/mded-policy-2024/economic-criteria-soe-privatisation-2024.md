STATE OF PLAY REGARDING KEY INDICATORS RECENT REFORM Government Decision No. 819/2023 regarding the approval of the triage Mechanism of state enterprises and a commercial companies with state capital ( https://www.legis.md/cautare/getResults?doc_id=139984&lang=ro ) Establishing a detailed and transparent assessment of state enterprises and state-owned companies according to the categories and criteria specified in the regulation. Classification of enterprises into categories based on criteria: Enterprises considered unsuitable for privatization: e nterprises that meet at least one of the following criteria: their core business is directly dependent on the use of publicly owned assets in the public domain (strategic infrastructure: airports, naval/river ports, telecommunications networks, electrical networks, etc.); they hold a state monopoly, natural monopoly or fiscal monopoly position; they operate activities on which national security directly depends; they provide/deliver services/goods of major importance in the absence of a market/ acute shortage of private economic operators; existence of long-term commitments from the state. Enterprises recommended for restructuring into a different legal form: the enterprise's purpose is not commercial, rather, it is to implement state policies. Enterprises suitable for privatization after restructuring/reorganization: Enterprises experiencing a consecutive minimum 5-year loss without positive dynamics; The ratio of assets to turnover is greater than 2:1; possession of valuable assets that can be separated from underperforming ones and monetized by the state Enterprises recommended for immediate privatization: entities with high levels of debt and/or the state being a minority shareholder; entities that compete with the private sector and not falling into other specified categories Enterprises recommended for liquidation: Net asset value is negative or reduced by more than half of the share capital.
