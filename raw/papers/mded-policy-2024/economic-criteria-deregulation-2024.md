### Notes to slide 2

Mission of the Economic Council: Facilitating dialogue between the business environment and policy makers in order to develop a favorable socioeconomic climate and a non discriminatory, transparent, and investment-friendly business environment. As the National Trade Facilitation Committee, the Council continuously monitors and evaluates progress in implementing the WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement, identifying innovative solutions to streamline procedures and reduce trade costs, thus aligning with international best practices and contributing to the enhancement of the national economy's competitiveness. Mission of the Working Group on Business Regulation: Optimizing the legal regulatory framework impacting the business environment and monitoring the activities of public administration authorities responsible for regulating entrepreneurial activities. Reviewing the regulatory impact analysis of draft normative acts concerning entrepreneurial activities, including specific drafts, in accordance with: Law No. 235/2006 on the Basic Principles of Regulating Entrepreneurial Activities; Law No. 160/2011 on Authorizing Entrepreneurial Activities; Law No. 161/2011 on the Single Window for Entrepreneurs; Law No. 131/2012 on State Control over Entrepreneurial Activities."
