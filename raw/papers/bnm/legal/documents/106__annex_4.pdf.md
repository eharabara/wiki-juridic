Attachment No.2 
to the Regulation on the issuance by the National Bank of Moldova 
of authorizations to export funds from the Republic of Moldova  
 
 
I. The list of documents to be attached  
to the application for issuing the authorization to export funds  
from the Republic of Moldova and the procedure of such documents submission  
 
1. The identity document of the resident individual in which the exit visa from the 
Republic of Moldova is applied by the authorized bodies of the Republic of Moldova (the 
passport, issued to the citizen of the Republic of Moldova or to a stateless person for the exist 
from /entry into the Republic of Moldova or the national passport of the foreign citizen). 
 
2. The identity document of the non-resident individual that applies for the NBM 
authorization. 
 
3. Documents confirming the holding, under the right of property, the amounts of funds 
requested to be authorized by the National Bank of Moldova for export from the Republic of 
Moldova (for instance, contract of real estate purchase and sale, contract of securities purchase 
and sale; certificate of inheritance etc.). 
 
4. Certificate on the absence or existence of arrears to the national public budget, issued by 
the fiscal authority of the Republic of Moldova on the name of the person establishing his /her 
permanent residence abroad. The specified certificate shall simultaneously comply with the 
following conditions: confirm the absence of arrears to the national public budget, be issued not 
earlier than 5 working days before the date of submission to the National Bank of Moldova of 
the application on issuing the authorization and not earlier than the day following the 
performance of property sale transaction or of other transactions, as a result of which the funds 
were received. 
 
5. Other documents, which individual considers necessary to submit in order to obtain the 
NBM authorization. 
 
 
II. Requirements for the documents specified under items 1 - 5 of this Attachment 
 
1. The document indicated under item 4 shall be submitted to and kept at the National 
Bank of Moldova in original. 
 
2. The documents indicated under items 1-3 and 5 shall be submitted to the National Bank 
of Moldova in original or copies, with the submission of their originals for verification. 
Following the verification made by the responsible officer of the National Bank of Moldova, the 
original documents shall be returned to the individual, while the authenticated copies shall be 
kept at the National Bank of Moldova.
