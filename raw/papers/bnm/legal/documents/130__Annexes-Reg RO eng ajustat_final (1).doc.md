      2.  In determining the base, the calculation  shall  not  include  the
balances of loans, deposits and pledged  deposits  in  MDL,  non-convertible
currencies  and  in  freely  convertible  currencies  obtained  from   banks
licensed by the National Bank and branches of foreign banks licensed by  the
National  Bank,  reflected  in  the  accounts  no.2062,  no.2075,   no.2095,
no.2096, no.2097, no.2098,  no.2331,  no.2332,  no.2341,  no.2342,  no.2343,
no.2344, no. 2346, no.2347, no.2348, no.2349,  the  balances  of  the  loans
obtained from the National Bank and the banks licensed by the National  Bank
and from the branches of the foreign banks licensed  by  the  National  Bank
through Repo  agreements  reflected  in  the  account  no.  2151,  the  debt
balances against banks  regarding  the  financial  lease  reflected  in  the
account no. 2531 and the balances of the  financial  debts  from  the  banks
licensed by the National Bank and from the branches  of  the  foreign  banks
licensed by the National Bank, reflected  in  the  accounts  no.  2571,  no.
2572.
      The credit balance of the accounts no.2181 and no.2183 will be
diminished by the debit balance of the account no.2186.
      For account number 2807 the calculation shall not include the  amounts
granted to the payer of bank loans in order to open and /  or  fill  in  the
letters of credit at his/her order.
      For accounts no. 2809 and no. 2818 the bank's own funds shall not be
included in the calculation.
      For account no. 2851 the calculation shall not include the amounts  of
deposits received from bank branches located in the Republic of Moldova.
