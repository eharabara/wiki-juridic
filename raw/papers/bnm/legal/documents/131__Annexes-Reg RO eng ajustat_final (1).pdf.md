Procedure for drawing up the Report on value of financial means attracted in Moldovan lei and non-
convertible currencies included in the calculation base and value of required reserves in Moldovan lei 
 
1. Information included in the report shall be divided in 2 tables: 
a) table ORD 2.2A Value of financial means attracted in Moldovan lei and non-convertible currencies included 
in the calculation base and value of required reserves in Moldovan lei   
Totals 
 
b) table ORD 2.2B Value of financial resourcess attracted in Moldovan lei and in non-convertible currencies re-
calculated in Moldovan lei included in the calculation base  
Breakdown by accounts 
 
2. Tables mentioned at item 1 shall indicate the following: 
a) for all rows, columns 1 to 31, shall indicate amounts of balances for all dates of observance period from 16th 
of the previous month – to the 15th of the current month;  
b) amounts of attracted financial means shall be reflected in integers; 
c) amounts in non-convertible currencies and freely convertible currencies (FCC) shall be recalculated in MDL 
and their equivalent in MDL shall be indicated in the report;   
d) recalculation in MDL shall be made according to the official exchange rate of the Moldovan lei valid for 
respective dates of the observance period included in report;    
e) amounts of financial means attracted in FCC recalculated in MDL shall be included in the report for 
information purposes;   
f) for the dates of weekends and holidays, the amounts of balances registered in the preceding working day shall 
be included; 
g) column 16 for the months with 30 calendar days and columns14 (15)-16 for February shall not be filled in. 
3. Average value of attracted financial means, shall be calculated as the sum of values within columns 1 to 31 
divided by the number of calendar days within the observance period.  
4. For describing the algorithm of completing the Report on amount of financial means attracted in Moldovan 
lei and non-convertible currencies included in the calculation base and value of required reserves in Moldovan 
lei, the following notes shall be included:      
MAtotald – financial means attracted in MDL, in NCC and FCC included in the calculation base, total, in 
balance on date „d”;  
MAmdl d – financial means attracted in MDL, included in the calculation base, total, in balance on date „d”; 
MAvn d – financial means attracted in NCC, included in the calculation base, total, in balance on date „d”; 
MAvlcd – financial means attracted in FCC, included in the calculation base, total, in balance on date „d”; 
MAt2d – financial means attracted in MDL and NCC with a maturity period > 2 years, which meet the criteria 
set out in item 15 of this Regulation, included in the calculation base, total, in balance on date „d”; 
MAcu d – financial means attracted in MDL and NCC, subject to reserving, in balance on date„d”; 
NromdlD – reserving ratio from financial means attracted in MDL and NCC, in force for the observance period 
D, in accordance with the NBM normative acts (%, with two digits after the decimal point);   
ROD – amount of required reserves attracted in MDL and NCC, included in the calculation base within the 
observance period D; 
ci - account number of “Liabilities” class, as indicated in Annex no. 1 of this Regulation;   
ci10d – total balance, in account ci on date „d”; 
ci11d – balance of financial means in MDL, in account ci, on date „d”; 
ci12d – balance of financial means in non-convertible currencies in account ci, on date „d”;   
ci13d – balance of financial means in FCC in account ci on date „d”; 
ci14d – balance of financial means in MDL and NCC, with a maturity period > 2 years, which meet the criteria 
set out in item 15 of this Regulation in account ci, on date „d”; 
ci15d – total balance of financial means in MDL and NCC, in account ci, on date „d”, subject to reserving. 
 
5. Table ORD 2.2A shows total information on financial means attracted by banks and value of required 
reserves in MDL calculated for the observance period. Table shall be filled in as follows:    
 


1)  Row 1.0: 
a) - for each date „d” (col. 1÷31), it shall be indicated MAtotal d = MAmdl d (row1.1) + MAvn d (row 1.2) 
+MAvlcd (row1.3); 
b) - column 32 shall not be filled in, (cell is empty). 
2) Row 1.1: 
a) - columns 1-31 shall be determined based on information from table ORD 2.2B; 
for each date „d” (col. 1÷31), it shall be indicated MAmdld = ∑ cells of the respective date „d” from rows of
