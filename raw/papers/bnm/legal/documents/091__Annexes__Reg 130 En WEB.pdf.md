regardless of the form of its conclusion that has as main objective or effect: concerted exercise of voting rights

control over the bank; concerted activity within the bank management or persons exercising control over it; 
or exercising the right to appoint the majority of the board members or executive body of the bank or persons 
exercising control over it;
