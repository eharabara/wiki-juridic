provision of the bank’s bylaws1:
1. I AM NOT ACTING IN CONCERT MANNER2 with none of the  direct  or  indirect
shareholders, including the beneficial owners of the shares of the bank.
In  the  event  that  I  decide  TO  ACT  IN  CONCERT  MANNER   with   other
shareholders:
a) I SHALL INFORM the National Bank  of  Moldova,  according  to  the  legal
provision, about any agreement, regardless of the  form  of  its  conclusion
that has as main objective or effect: concerted exercise  of  voting  rights
within the general meetings of the bank’s  shareholders  or  within  general
meetings of persons that exercise control over the bank; concerted  activity
within the bank  management  or  persons  exercising  control  over  it;  or
exercising the right to  appoint  the  majority  of  the  board  members  or
executive body of the bank or persons exercising control over it;
b) I SHALL REQUIRE THE WRITTEN PERMISSION of National  Bank  of  Moldova  in
the cases provided by law.
