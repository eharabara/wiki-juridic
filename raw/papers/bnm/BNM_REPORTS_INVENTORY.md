# BNM report series — official-site inventory

## Scope
This inventory covers BNM’s formal report-series catalogues that are explicitly labelled as reports on the English BNM site: Annual Reports, Inflation Reports, and Financial Stability Reports. It does **not** attempt to enumerate the interactive statistical database, recurring statistical tables, press releases, or one-off analytical documents that may also be called ‘reports’ in ordinary language.

**Inventory total:** 25 annual report entries, 61 inflation report entries, 7 financial stability report entries — 93 records.

Source catalogues: [Annual](https://www.bnm.md/en/categories/raport-anual) · [Inflation](https://www.bnm.md/en/tipuri-de-publicatii/raport-asupra-inflatiei) · [Financial stability](https://www.bnm.md/en/tipuri-de-publicatii/raportul-de-stabilitate-financiara).
