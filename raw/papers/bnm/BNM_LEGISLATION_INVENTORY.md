# BNM mandate-related legislation — official-site inventory

## Scope and method
This is a direct inventory of the National Bank of Moldova (BNM) English-language **List of laws** and **Regulations list**. It preserves BNM’s own five regulatory domains. It is an index of BNM-hosted/currently-listed instruments, not an assertion that every instrument is currently in force or the legally authentic consolidation. For legal reliance, check the Official Monitor / Legis.md and the amendment or repeal status in each underlying BNM record.

Primary registers: [List of laws](https://www.bnm.md/en/content/list-laws) · [Regulations list](https://www.bnm.md/en/content/regulations-list).

**Inventory total:** 16 laws and 99 subordinate instruments (115 entries).
