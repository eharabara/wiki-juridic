# Overview

The European Commission’s October 2024 proposals comprise COM(2024) 469 final, proposing a Regulation establishing a Reform and Growth Facility for Moldova, and COM(2024) 470 final, the Communication on the Moldova Growth Plan. Together they describe the EU-side facility and the Growth Plan’s strategic rationale. ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/eu-reform-growth-facility-moldova-2024.md] ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/eu-moldova-growth-plan-communication-2024.md]

## Relationship to the Moldovan reform agenda

The EU facility and the Moldovan [[HG-260-2025|Agenda de reforme aferentă Planului de creștere 2025–2027]] are related but not interchangeable: the former is the proposed EU financing and governance instrument; the latter is the Moldovan adopted reform agenda. The distinction is necessary for sound analysis of conditions, financing, accountability and implementation.
