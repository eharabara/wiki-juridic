# Overview

The Economic Reform Programme (ERP) 2024–2026, issued by the Ministry of Finance in January 2024, provides a macroeconomic, fiscal and structural-reform framework for the programme period. ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/economic-reform-programme-2024-2026.md]

## Role in EU economic-policy dialogue

The ERP provides a structured bridge between macro-fiscal policy, structural reforms and the EU’s economic-policy dialogue. It is an important reference for [[criterii-economice-aderare-ue-moldova|economic criteria]], while [[HG-260-2025|the Growth Plan agenda]] is a later and more specific reform-and-investment instrument.

## Evidence discipline

Treat programme forecasts, policy intentions, fiscal plans and implementation results as separate categories. Later official reports may supersede the programme’s forecasts or stated status.
