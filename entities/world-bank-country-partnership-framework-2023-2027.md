# Overview

The World Bank Group Country Partnership Framework (CPF) for Moldova for FY2023–FY2027 sets the partnership framework of IBRD, IFC and MIGA. It is a primary donor-programming source rather than a national strategic act. ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/world-bank-country-partnership-framework-2023-2027.md]

## Relevance to Moldova policy work

The CPF helps map development-finance priorities and the World Bank Group’s potential contribution to reforms, investment and institutional capacity. It should be read alongside [[world-bank-country-economic-memorandum-2023|the CEM]], [[economic-reform-programme-2024-2026|ERP 2024–2026]] and [[HG-260-2025|the Growth Plan reform agenda]].

## Analytical use

Separate CPF objectives and proposed support from budgeted interventions, adopted legislation, completed reforms and observed results.
