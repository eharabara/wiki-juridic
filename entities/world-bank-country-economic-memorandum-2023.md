# Overview

The World Bank’s 2023 Moldova Country Economic Memorandum (CEM), *Achieving the European Union’s standard of living within a generation*, is a diagnostic source on Moldova’s long-term convergence challenge. It is analytical evidence, not an adopted Moldovan policy instrument. ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/world-bank-country-economic-memorandum-2023.md]

## Relevance to policy programming

The CEM provides an external analytical baseline for interpreting the ambitions of [[HG-393-2024|SNDE 2030]], [[HG-260-2025|Agenda de reforme aferentă Planului de creștere]] and [[economic-reform-programme-2024-2026|ERP 2024–2026]]. It should be used to distinguish structural diagnosis from government commitments and reported implementation.

## Evidence use

Use the report for constraints, comparative diagnosis and long-run policy questions; verify any current statistic against a newer primary statistical or institutional source before using it as a current fact.
