# Overview

*Foaia de parcurs pentru finanțare durabilă, Republica Moldova 2024–2028* presents a national framework, key pillars and an action plan for sustainable-finance development. ^[raw/papers/mded-policy-2024/foaia-de-parcurs-finantare-durabila-2024-2028.md]

## Policy role

The roadmap is a policy-framework source for linking climate and environmental objectives to financial-sector development, investment mobilisation and institutional action. It should be read with [[finantare-climatica-si-durabila-in-moldova|the climate- and sustainable-finance concept]], [[HG-393-2024|SNDE 2030]] and the relevant financial-sector legal framework.

## Evidence use

The roadmap establishes intended direction and actions. It does not itself demonstrate delivery, market uptake, emissions effects or financial-system outcomes.
